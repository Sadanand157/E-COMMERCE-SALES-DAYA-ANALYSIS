# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing **E-commerce sales data** to uncover meaningful business insights related to **sales, profit, quantity, orders, and profit margin**.
An interactive dashboard has been created to help stakeholders track performance, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

The dashboard provides **year-over-year (YoY) growth analysis**, category-wise performance, top-selling products, and regional insights.

## ❓ Business Questions 

This dashboard is designed to answer key **business-driven questions** that help stakeholders understand performance and customer behavior:

1. **Compare Sales and Orders**
   * Compared total **sales value and number of orders** using a **single combined chart** to analyze demand vs revenue trends.
2. **Which Month Has the Highest Sales and Orders?**
   * Identified the **peak-performing month** by analyzing monthly sales and order volume trends.
3. **Who Purchased More – Men or Women?**
   * Analyzed purchase behavior by **gender** to determine which group contributes more to total orders and sales.
4. **What Are the Different Order Statuses in 2022?**
   * Evaluated order distribution across statuses such as:
     * Delivered
     * Returned
     * Cancelled
     * Pending
5. **Top 10 States Contributing to Sales**
   * Ranked the **top 10 states** based on total sales contribution to identify high-performing regions.
6. **Relationship Between Age and Gender Based on Orders**
   * Analyzed how **age groups and gender** impact the number of orders to understand customer demographics.
7. **Which Channel Contributes the Maximum Sales?**
   * Identified the most profitable **sales channel** contributing the highest revenue.
8. **Highest Selling Category**
   * Determined which **product category** generates the maximum sales value.
9. **Percentage of Total Orders Delivered**
   * Calculated the **delivery success rate** by finding the percentage of delivered orders out of total orders.
